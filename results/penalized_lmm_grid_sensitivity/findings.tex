\paragraph{Sensitivity to the lower end of the penalty path.}
Because 11 primary selections occur at the path endpoint, we repeat native tuning on all 46 stored inputs after adding the last penalty divided by 10 and 100, and zero, to each original grid. The hierarchy parameter, group splits, convergence gates and loss are unchanged; this is a secondary analysis motivated by the endpoint diagnostic, not a new independent study. All 46/46 analyses complete; 9 choose an added penalty, 4 choose zero and 3 change selected predictor sets. On cake the extended native MSE is 62.974, and the post-selection ML MSE is 63.055. The extended native mean-MSE values are 0.0827 for ordinary signals and 0.0818 for suppression, with FDR 0.388 and 0.737. Both suppressed predictors are recovered in 19/20 cases. Primary and extended-grid outputs are retained separately; the sensitivity check does not optimize over other hierarchy parameters or establish uniform superiority.
