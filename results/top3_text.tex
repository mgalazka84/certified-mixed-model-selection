A separate diagnostic refitted the three highest-scoring GLS candidates and selected the best among them. This fixed top-three heuristic also agreed with all 35 comparator choices. Its median screening-to-heuristic time ratio was 0.602 over the pilot, but the certificate method was slower in the difficult subsets: median ratios were 1.51 for near ties, 1.35 for the complete null, and 1.13 for group-aligned predictors. This is not the full published ARSS algorithm: it has no significance-based expansion or pair rescue. It was timed in a separate run, so these ratios are descriptive overhead diagnostics rather than matched performance estimates. The formal exclusion guarantee is the proposed distinction; universal speed superiority over a short heuristic is not claimed.
