In the suppressor scenario, both active members of the pair were recovered in 50/50 verified runs, 0/50 local-single runs and 50/50 ARSS-JPS runs. The paired population-mean MSE difference, verified minus singles, was -0.360 with Monte Carlo standard error 0.007. This contrast assesses the benefit of the pair policy; it does not isolate a statistical benefit from numerical certification.

Across jointly completed datasets, median verified/ARSS-JPS elapsed time was 2.60. The archived shortlist policy and the all-family verifier therefore have different measured costs as well as different guarantees and stopping thresholds. The numerical evidence should not be summarized as universal superiority of one selector.
